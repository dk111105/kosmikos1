About the creator

Divya is a 19 year old Computer Science student, studying in Women's Christian College, Chennai. The lack of interactive and intuitive social media platforms that are professional, yet fun inspired her to create Kosmikos. The ingenuienty and simplicity of Tumblr and MySpace drove her to make the website similar to something that you might've found on your home computer from the 2000's but better.
